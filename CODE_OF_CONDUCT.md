# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in `superior-skills`, regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- communicate with empathy, clarity, and respect;
- critique ideas and changes without attacking people;
- welcome questions, corrections, and different levels of experience;
- give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty;
- respect privacy, consent, attribution, and project boundaries;
- accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm where possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate humiliation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation, deceptive attribution, or knowingly false allegations;
- encouraging others to violate this Code of Conduct;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository hosting platform's moderation or maintainer-contact channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Include enough context for a fair review: what happened, where it occurred, relevant dates or links, and any immediate safety concern. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, subject to the need to investigate and respond.

For security vulnerabilities rather than conduct concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close or lock discussions, issue a correction or warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant. The response should be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, prior context, and risk of recurrence.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest should not lead the review. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this Code of Conduct; an unsubstantiated good-faith report does not.
